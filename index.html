The Tee

A Crown Built To Move With Your Swing

Every Regal Tee is topped with a flexible, 8-prong crown that gives your ball room to seat naturally and pivot through impact instead of fighting a rigid post. The result is a cleaner release off the face — built for drives that go longer and land where you aimed.

  • Flexible 8-prong crown cradles the ball at address
  • 75°–105° adjustable pivot absorbs impact
  • Engineered for longer, more precise drives
  • Durable construction, designed and made in America
